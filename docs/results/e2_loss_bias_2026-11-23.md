# E2 result and the DINO-loss roll bias (2026-11-23)

Evaluation only (ground truth used for scoring). Single splat `tool_table`, POGS paper variant.

## 1. E2 (40 trials, 1,120 frames; `data_results/e2_info/summary.md`)
| Quantity | At estimate | At GT | Pre-registered pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spearman(log roll information, abs roll error) | +0.22 | −0.20 | ≤ −0.5 → **FAIL** |
| Weakest rotation direction within 15° of bit axis | 17% | 27% | ≥ 70% → **FAIL** |
| Median share of bit axis in the 2-D weak subspace | 1.00 | 1.00 | ≥ 0.9 (definitional complement) → pass |
| Median roll information / strongest rotation information | 0.144 | 0.102 | – |
| loss(estimate) < loss(GT) | 87–100% of frames by phase | | – |

**P1 fails.** Roll is always inside the weak rotation subspace, but the information does not predict the size of the
roll error, and the loss is lower at the (wrong) estimate than at the truth on most frames.

## 2. Why: the DINO feature term is biased in roll (1-D sweeps, `grasp_+15_s0`, frame 0)
The drill rests at exactly its scanned pose (GT rotation 0.00°). Loss along roll about the bit axis, from the GT pose,
2° grid over ±30°:

| Loss | Minimum at | L(min) − L(0) | Range over ±30° |
|---|---|---|---|
| POGS total (DINO + 3.7·depth) | −10° (frame 0), −12° (frame 3) | −0.0020 / −0.0025 | 0.031 |
| DINO only | **−10°** | −0.0018 | 0.029 |
| depth only | −2° (≈ 0, within grid) | 0.0000 | 0.0012 |
| RGB only | **0°** | 0.0000 | 0.0105 |

The tracker converges to the biased minimum (estimated roll −6.8° at frame 0, −12.4° by frame 3), which is the
12.5 ± 0.2° floor seen in E1 across 200 trials. Depth is unbiased but too flat to counteract DINO; RGB has a sharp
minimum at the truth.

## 3. Consequence for the plan
- The roll floor is a **feature-loss bias**, not weak observability of an unbiased loss and not an optimisation
  failure (plan rev. 2 decision table: "loss at estimate lower → features or loss mismatch; test alternatives before
  anything else").
- An information-adaptive filter (M1–M4) models variance, not bias, so it cannot remove this floor. P2 (E3) can still
  be run but its motivation in the roll story is gone.
- Next experiment (config only, `TrackingConfig.use_rgb`): POGS + RGB term on O4 (E2b). Further candidates: a
  stronger depth weight, DINOv3 / higher feature resolution, and checking the feature-field fit of the drill splat.

Caveats: two frames of one trial for §2 (the E1 table shows the same 12.5 ± 0.2° on all 200 trials, so the bias is
systematic); synthetic renderer, where RGB is cleaner than on a real camera (POGS's real-robot ablation found RGB-only
tracking fails, Table I).
