## 3. Verdict on P0 (≥ 46/50 in at least one setting under the paper-faithful rule)
**P0 FAILS.** Best: POGS +30°, 39/50, Wilson [0.65, 0.87] ("inconclusive" against 80%, below the 46/50 needed
for a lower bound ≥ 0.80). The supervisor's 80% target is **not met** in simulation even under the paper's own
rule. The rule mismatch explains a large part of the shortfall (2→32, 14→25, 13→39, 5→17), not all of it.

## 4. What remains after removing roll (E1)
- **The 10–24° floor is roll about the bit axis**: median roll share of the pre-perturbation geodesic error
  0.94 for POGS (|roll| 9.5 ± 2.8°, swing 4.0 ± 3.4°). It is already 12.5 ± 0.2° at the end of the approach
  phase, with the drill on the table and no gripper contact, so it comes from initialisation / weak roll
  observability, **not gripper occlusion**.
- **Residual swing near the bar**: swing at N is 4.1–4.6° for the + settings, so the 5° threshold still
  decides about half of the failures.
- **Sign asymmetry is a systematic bias**: the pre-perturbation signed error of POGS about the vertical
  (perturbation) axis is positive in 167/200 trials. A + perturbation then moves the truth toward the biased
  estimate and a − one away from it, consistent with + settings scoring higher under the absolute rule too
  (32 vs 25; 39 vs 17). Negative settings also have heavy-tailed losses (swing at N 9.5 ± 16.7°,
  13.4 ± 20.5°). The post-hoc relative rule additionally leaks: Static passes it only when its error is
  aligned (7–9 of 21–29) and never when opposed (0 of 21–29).
- **The fixed-R KF does not help** (28 vs 32, 35 vs 39, 24 vs 25, 18 vs 17).

## 5. Decision (plan rev. 2, §8 stop rules)
- The floor is roll observability / initialisation, not optimisation failure (E2 smoke: loss(estimate) <
  loss(GT) on 95–100% of frames). The method line continues to E2 / G1.
- The ± gap is partly a signed-bias artefact; it must be reported as such and controlled in E3 (stratify by
  the sign of the pre-perturbation yaw error).
- Message for the supervisor: "Under our stricter rule the 80% target is not met (2–28%). Under the paper's
  own rule (roll free) POGS reaches 34–78% (best 39/50 at +30°), still below 80%. The residual is a ~4° tool-axis
  error and a systematic yaw bias that favours one perturbation direction. The rotation error itself is roll
  about the drill bit, present before the gripper touches the drill."

Caveats: one trained splat (no splat-seed variance yet, T7); simulator jaws not collision-checked; synthetic.

Accurate wording: **under its own rule, POGS reproduces the paper's 30° tier in one direction (+30°: 39 vs 40/50)

rotation as ±30° at a smaller angle. So the 15° comparison is the weaker one. "G0 fails" remains the
pre-registered verdict (P0 asked for ≥ 46/50 in one setting).
