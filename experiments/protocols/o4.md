# Protocol O4: in-grasp tool perturbation recovery rate, target 80 % (DRAFT)

Status: **draft**. Freeze before the first O4 run.

## Scene
- `tool_table` scene and checkpoint from O3. The benchmark gripper (`make_robot_gripper`,
  scale 1.0: it is sized for drills) grasps the drill handle and lifts it 10 cm.
- In-grasp perturbation: the drill rotates inside the closed gripper about the grasp axis by
  ±15° or ±30° (CW / CCW) within one frame, then the gripper holds still for 15 frames.
  Gripper visible throughout (occluder), as in the paper's Table II setup.

## Trials
- 4 settings (+15, −15, +30, −30) × 50 trials. Trial i: seed i, random start yaw, random
  placement, random lift height ∈ [8, 12] cm. Same trials for every method.

## Success (fixed in advance)
- Tool tip frame (`tool_tip_frame`) within 3 cm and 5° of GT at frame N = 10 after the
  perturbation (2 s at 5 Hz), and never lost (> 10 cm) after it.

## Report
- Recovery rate + Wilson 95 % CI per setting and method; verdict via
  `metrics.stats.target_verdict(k, 50, 0.80)`: met / not met / inconclusive.
- Paper's 48/50 (15°) and 40/50 (30°) shown as physical-robot reference only.
